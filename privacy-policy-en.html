Privacy Policy — Me Guarde

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the Me Guarde app ("the App"), developed by Paulo Bernardes, collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Who we are

Me Guarde is an Android app developed independently by Paulo Bernardes. For privacy-related questions, please contact us at: pberna.dev@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

The App itself does not collect or store personal data on our servers. However, we use third-party services that may collect certain information:

  • Google AdMob — displays advertisements to free-tier users. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (GAID) and usage data to personalize ads. Premium users are not shown any advertisements.
  • Google Play Billing — securely processes in-app payments. We do not have access to your credit card or payment method details.

3. Advertising consent (GDPR)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the App displays a consent screen on first launch, in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive. You may withdraw or change your consent at any time in the App's Settings.

Users who purchase Me Guarde Premium are not subject to advertising, and therefore no advertising data is collected.

4. How we use the information

The information collected by third-party services is used exclusively to:

  • Display relevant advertisements (free users only)
  • Process and verify in-app purchases
  • Improve the App's performance

5. Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties beyond the services mentioned in this policy. The third-party services we use have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy

6. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device, privately and securely:

  • Your Premium purchase status (if applicable)
  • Your advertising consent preferences

This data is not transmitted to our servers.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are a resident of the EEA, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access, rectify, and erase your data
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your country's data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at: pberna.dev@gmail.com

8. Security

We adopt appropriate technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

9. Children's data protection

The App is rated suitable for all ages and its content is of a spiritual and cultural nature. However, we do not intentionally collect advertising data from children under the age of 13. If you become aware that advertising data from a child has been collected, please contact us at pberna.dev@gmail.com so that we can take appropriate action.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We recommend checking it regularly. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when it was last revised.

11. Contact
